## Cables: key well / function row (13-pin flat flex, 1.25 mm pitch)

Only needed if the damaged flex unplugs at both ends; on the Advantage 2 the key
well flex is normally part of the key well. Measure the old one first: length,
and whether the contacts are on the same side at both ends (Type A) or opposite
sides (Type D). DigiKey lists 15168-xx13 as "top on both sides" = check before buying.

## Cables: thumb clusters (10-wire, 2.54 mm, single-row 1x10 both ends)

Pillz thumb PCB uses a 1x10 single-row header, not 2x5 IDC. Either a ready
10-wire Dupont female-female ribbon (peel 10 off a 40-wire strip) or KF2510
housings + crimp pins + ribbon. 10 cm is enough (upstream says >= 50 mm).
